# Fraud Takeover

Treat a safe-account story, pressured release fee, impersonation or credential exposure as concrete warning evidence. Prioritise stopping the risky proposed action and an urgent bank fraud/security handoff; specify which action is on hold. Use a known trusted bank contact route, not one supplied by the suspected scammer. Never seek codes or facilitate another transfer to test safety.

Distinguish an authorised payment induced by deception from an unauthorised transaction. Neither a high amount nor an annoyed customer alone proves fraud. A failed identity check alone may need secure recovery, without asserting account takeover as fact.

PSR describes APP scams and bank reporting/investigation. Reimbursement depends on scheme, timing, eligibility and the investigation; do not promise it, apply a blanket exclusion, blame the customer or quote an unverified deadline. State separately any urgent protection still needing execution.
