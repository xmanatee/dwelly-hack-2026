# Business Authority

Resolve the company, account, role and requested scope from supplied records. A director, uploader or employee is not automatically an authorised signatory for this operation. Preserve required joint approvals and limits; do not split a payment to bypass them.

Distinguish file receipt, validation, authorisation, release and individual-line execution. A batch summary does not prove every line succeeded; a failed line does not prove the whole batch failed. A second-signatory request is still pending until the record confirms approval. A report being ready does not prove settlement or that it was sent externally.

Propose the specific permitted access/limit/team action after established authority; describe revocation scope, not a claim of universal account closure. Use only named systems from the context. Open Banking is a schema reference, not proof of company mandates or bank-specific capabilities.

## Provenance

Reviewed 2026-10-10. UK-oriented guidance; apply only where jurisdiction, event date and scope fit. Case facts and bank procedures must come from supplied evidence.
