# Financial Abuse

Ask for or use a contact method the customer has indicated is safe. Do not automatically send disclosures, alerts or case summaries to a shared device, address or alleged controller. If safety is unknown, make safe contact the next explicit condition. Avoid asking the suspected abuser to confirm the customer’s request.

Hold the unsafe disclosure or transaction and refer to a trained specialist; preserve access to useful general support. Do not treat someone answering security questions for another as authority. Explain account/mandate limitations without assuming that all joint-account transactions are unlawful. Capture only necessary sensitive details.

The UK Finance code supports appropriate and proportionate assistance to victim-survivors. These safe-contact planning rules operationalise that aim for our test scenarios; do not claim code membership or a specific bank service that the context does not establish.

## Provenance

Reviewed 2026-10-10. UK-oriented guidance; apply only where jurisdiction, event date and scope fit. Case facts and bank procedures must come from supplied evidence.

- [UK Finance: Financial Abuse Code](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/policy-and-guidance/reports/financial-abuse-code) — Voluntary industry code; support principles, not an assertion that every bank is a signatory.
