# Complaints Rights

Handle the operational query and the complaint as separate work when both are present. Do not make complaint registration conditional on abandoning the dispute or accepting a refund. Include a concrete complaint/team action and only the acknowledgements, references or status actually available.

Preserve the complaint’s scope, relevant chronology and requested remedy. A review request does not establish a fee reversal or compensation; a bank’s refusal is not proof that no further remedy exists. Explain unresolved points and the applicable escalation route.

The Ombudsman considers individual facts, relevant rules and account terms; deadlines vary by complaint type. Do not invent a single response period for every case or imply a final Ombudsman outcome before review. General factual information is useful; personalised legal/investment advice is outside this planner’s role.

## Provenance

Reviewed 2026-10-10. UK-oriented guidance; apply only where jurisdiction, event date and scope fit. Case facts and bank procedures must come from supplied evidence.
