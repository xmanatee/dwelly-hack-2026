# Cards Access

Separate lost-card protection, replacement ordering, dispatch and arrival; one does not establish another. For a declined or restricted card use the recorded reason and status, not a guessed cause. Route unrecognised transactions or disclosed credentials to fraud protection as well as the ordinary card process.

Never request a full PIN, password or one-time code in a reply. Use the bank’s supported recovery process. Device linking is not established by app installation; restored access is not an automatic unblock of every feature. Caller ID, a supplied name and matching contact records identify candidates, not authenticated people. A verified session may establish identity, but verify its scope for sensitive changes. A new phone number cannot authenticate its own change.

For closure identify existing obligations and the applicable closure workflow; do not claim closure from a request alone. For routine status information already provided, reply directly without inventing another identity challenge or lookup. These are project handling rules; exact recovery and change procedures come from supplied bank policy.
