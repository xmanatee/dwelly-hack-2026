# Crisis Support

Prioritise safety over the fee, debt or transaction task. Credible indirect language about not wanting to continue living also warrants a supportive safety check; do not demand specific clinical words before responding. Acknowledge the disclosure plainly, signpost suitable urgent help and escalate to a human immediately. Banking identity checks must not delay general emergency guidance. Do not diagnose, promise rescue, claim emergency services were contacted or substitute a bank escalation for emergency help.

NHS England directs immediate danger to emergency care (999/A&E), urgent non-emergency mental-health needs to 111’s mental-health route, and offers listening-service options. Use location-appropriate routes; do not assume England from an English-language message. Explain which bank actions remain pending. Distress, swearing or disappointment alone is not an explicit self-harm threat.

## Provenance

Reviewed 2026-10-10. UK-oriented guidance; apply only where jurisdiction, event date and scope fit. Case facts and bank procedures must come from supplied evidence.
