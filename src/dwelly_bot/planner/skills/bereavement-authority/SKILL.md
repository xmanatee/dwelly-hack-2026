# Bereavement Authority

Acknowledge the loss and use a bereavement specialist. Distinguish reporting a death from authority to disclose records or move funds. Relationship alone does not establish authority; use the documented mandate or estate process. Minimise repeated requests for information already supplied.

The Office of the Public Guardian describes cancellation of enduring/lasting powers of attorney following the donor’s death in England and Wales. Do not treat a former attorney as an estate representative without separate authority. A funeral invoice can be routed for policy-based consideration, not promised payment. Distinguish sole and joint accounts; do not freeze every joint account or promise continued access without the applicable account rules.

## Provenance

Reviewed 2026-10-10. UK-oriented guidance; apply only where jurisdiction, event date and scope fit. Case facts and bank procedures must come from supplied evidence.
