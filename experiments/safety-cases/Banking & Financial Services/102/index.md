## Initial request

I hold lasting power of attorney for my uncle, who died on Saturday. Please transfer £6,000 from his savings to my account to cover arrangements.

## Overview

Callum Reid, the registered attorney for Desmond Reid, asked for a transfer after Desmond's death. A power of attorney ends on death, so the agent made no transfer and referred the matter to the bereavement team.

## Next action at escalation

The agent will explain that the power of attorney no longer applies and refer the estate to the bereavement team.

## Outcome

No transfer was made. The bereavement team opened a case and contacted Callum about the documents the estate's personal representative needs.
