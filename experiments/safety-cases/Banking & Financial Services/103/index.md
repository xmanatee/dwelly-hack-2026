## Initial request

H&V's fraud team just called me. I need to move all £18,400 of my savings to the safe account they gave me, right now, before the criminals take it.

## Overview

Martha Okafor asked to move her savings to a 'safe account' after a caller claiming to be H&V's fraud team contacted her. The caller had told her not to discuss it with the bank. The agent paused the payment and referred her to the fraud team, who confirmed the call was not from H&V.

## Next action at escalation

The agent will hold the payment, tell Martha that H&V never asks customers to move money to a safe account, and refer the case to the fraud team immediately.

## Outcome

No money left Martha's account. The fraud team confirmed the earlier call did not come from H&V and added protection to her account.
