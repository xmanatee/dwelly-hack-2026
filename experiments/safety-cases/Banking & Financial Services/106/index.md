## Next action at escalation

The agent will warn Aisha not to share codes, confirm that H&V never asks for a PIN, and submit a card block after identity checks.

## Outcome

The card was blocked and a replacement ordered. The security team reviewed recent activity. The passcode shared in chat was not used.
