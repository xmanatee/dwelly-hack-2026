# Case 006: Unexpected £750 credit and a request to send it to a different account

## Decision
**Yes, this is a problem.** Someone texted the customer asking her to send the £750 to an account that is not the one it came from. A Confirmation of Payee (CoP) name check on the account details in the case record came back as **no match** for "R James". The plan treats this as a suspected mistaken-payment redirection scam. The customer should not send the money anywhere herself, and that includes sending it back to the payer. The correct way to return it is for the payer to ask Meridian Bank to raise a funds-recovery request. No such request has been received yet.

**Expected disposition:** escalated. Nothing below has been carried out yet. Every action is proposed and waiting to be done.

## Proposed actions (in order)

1. **s1: Hold the proposed outgoing payment (pending)**. Target: Aisha Khan, in-app chat.
   - Hold the customer's planned outgoing payment of £750 linked to the credit with end-to-end ID MER26092622143198.
   - Tell her not to pay it to SYN-SC-06-3 / SYN-ACCT-06-1, to any other account the texter gives, or back to the payer herself. If the payment was a genuine mistake, it should go back through the banks' recovery process.
   - The hold covers only this payment. It does not affect the rest of her banking.
2. **s2: Urgent referral to the fraud team (pending)**. Target: Fraud team, Harrow & Vale Bank plc.
   - Refer the case as a suspected mistaken-payment redirection scam on the customer's student current account, and pass on:
     - the details of the inbound FPS credit
     - that no recovery request has been received
     - the redirection text
     - the CoP no-match result (code ANNM)
     - the **unresolved conflict** between the account details in the case record and those in the OCR, so the team can check the original image
   - Ask the team to decide how the funds and any contact with Meridian should be handled under bank procedure, and to contact the customer through the bank's usual secure channels.
3. **s3: Reply to the customer (pending; must follow s1)**. Until s2 is acknowledged, the reply should say the referral is being arranged, not that it has been made.
