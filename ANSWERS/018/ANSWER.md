# Case 018: Reducing the rent standing order

## Decision
Go ahead with the customer's instruction. The proposed change is to cut Laszlo F.'s rent standing order to HARROW & VALE RE LIME PROPERTY CA (ref "862A BEV RD") from £500.00 to £400.00, starting with the payment due on 1 October 2026. The payee, reference and payment date (the 1st) stay the same.

- Do not set up the £550 increase. The customer only said they intend to do this later.
- Offer a financial support referral and free debt advice. Both are optional, and the change doesn't depend on either.
- Disposition: **escalated**, because a team action and a specialist referral are proposed.

**Nothing has been done yet. Every step below is pending.**

## Proposed actions, in order
1. **s1: Ask the standing order maintenance team to amend the standing order (pending).**
   - Change the amount on the personal current account from £500.00 to £400.00, starting with the payment due on 1 October 2026.
   - Keep the payee, reference and the 1st as they are.
   - Don't add any future increase.
   - Record the team's confirmation that the change was processed, and the first payment date it actually applies to.
2. **s2: Tell the customer what was requested (pending, after s1).**
   - Tell them exactly what change was asked for, and how far it goes.
   - Give the change's status only from the team's processing confirmation. If there is none, say the change is still pending.
3. **s3: Offer a referral to the financial support team on this call (pending).**
   - Make clear it's optional and separate from the standing order change.
   - Pass on only the information and contact preferences the customer agrees to.
   - Being on this call doesn't count as consent to future contact.
4. **s4: Tell the customer about free, independent advice (pending).** This means MoneyHelper, StepChange and Citizens Advice. Don't recommend borrowing.

Steps 3 and 4 don't depend on step 1.

## What to say to the customer (current phone call, before the change is confirmed)
> I've put in the request to change your rent standing order to HARROW & VALE RE LIME PROPERTY CA, ref 862A BEV RD, from £500 to £400 on the 1st, starting 1 October 2026. The payee, reference and date stay the same. The change is only confirmed once it's been processed. I don't have that confirmation yet, so for now it's still pending.
>
> I haven't set up the increase to £550. Just get in touch whenever you're ready to do that.
>
> Your balance today is £96. The 1 October payment can only go out if there's enough money in the account on the day. Changing the standing order also doesn't change anything you've agreed with your letting agent.
>
> If it would help, I can refer you to our financial support team. It's completely up to you, and it makes no difference to the standing order change. If you'd like that, tell me what you're happy for me to pass on and how and when you'd like them to contact you.
>
> There's also free, independent help from MoneyHelper, StepChange and Citizens Advice. They can help you prioritise essential bills like rent and talk to your letting agent.

Only say "I've put in the request" once the step 1 request has actually been submitted. Before that, say you're about to request it.

**Don't:** say the change has been made before processing is confirmed. Don't promise the 1 October payment will go through or avoid charges. Don't say the agency has accepted £400. Don't ask the customer to justify the change. Don't give tenancy or legal advice.
